How to order

Pick up the phone, or come by in person.

Our stock changes every week, so we don't sell online. A two-minute phone call gets you a straight answer about what we have now and what suits you.

  1. Call or stop in

    Tell us what you smoke, or what you're celebrating. If you're not sure, describe what you enjoy and we'll suggest something.

  2. We check the humidor

    We'll tell you what's on hand right now, singles or full boxes. If you can't come in straight away, we can set it aside for you.

  3. Collect, or we deliver

    Pick up at the shop, on the 3rd floor of One Lane Hill. Staying on the island? We can deliver to your hotel or guesthouse.

Buying a gift, or stocking up for an event? Call a few days ahead and we'll have the right boxes ready for you.

Our story

Why forty pirates?

Cuarenta Bucaneros is Spanish for “forty pirates.” Our founders wanted a name that said the business was meant to be fun. As they put it, it was the closest they dared get to “forty thieves.”

The business started in the early 2000s, after a work trip to Cuba. Bermuda retailers struggled to get a steady supply of real Cuban cigars at the time, so the founders went to the source. After about a year of checks by the Cuban authorities, they were approved to import Habanos directly. Our cigars have come straight from Cuba ever since, with as little handling as possible along the way.
