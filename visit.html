Questions

Good to know

Can I order online?

No. Cuban supply changes too quickly for an online shop to be accurate, and we'd rather give you a straight answer. Call +1 (441) 295 4523 during opening hours, or come in. You can also email cigars@cigarbox.bm, but for stock questions a phone call is quickest.

Can you hold something for me?

Yes. Call and tell us what you'd like, and we'll set it aside so you can collect it.

Do you deliver to hotels?

Yes. If you're staying in Bermuda, we can deliver to your hotel or guesthouse. Call to arrange a time.

Do you sell single cigars, or only boxes?

Both, depending on what's in stock. Singles are a good way to try something new before you buy a box.

Can I take Cuban cigars home with me?

That depends on your home country's customs rules, and they vary a lot. Some countries limit how many cigars you can bring in duty-free. Please check with your own customs authority before you travel.

Do you supply hotels, restaurants or events?

Yes. As well as selling to individual customers, we supply hotels, restaurants and private events in Bermuda. Call us to talk about what you need.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. We sell tobacco only to adults aged 18 and over, and we may ask for ID.
